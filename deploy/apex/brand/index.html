ACCENT — BOTH O'S

The O's alternate — white, accent, white, accent — which gives the word a rhythm a single coloured letter cannot. They are also the roundest shapes, so they hold a colour without the hairlines muddying it.

NOTES

Filled paths, not a typeface — nothing to load, no platform substitution, no licence.

Mono uses currentColor, so it takes any chain's accent. Two-tone defaults to var(--chain-accent-ui), the derived readable variant — never the raw brand hex, since four chain accents fail 4.5:1 and this is text.

Contrast is the idea and the constraint. Below ~18px the hairlines thin out — use the glyph for favicons and very small nav rows.
