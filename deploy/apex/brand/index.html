NOTES

One accented letter, not two. Two split the eye and the word read as decorated; a single accent at the end works like a full stop.

Filled paths, not a typeface — nothing to load, no platform substitution, no licence. Mono uses currentColor, so it takes any chain's accent.

Contrast is the idea and the constraint. Below ~18px the hairlines thin out — use the glyph for favicons and very small nav rows.
