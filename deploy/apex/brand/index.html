Construction

Every letter is built from the same 22-unit square cell — the O a ring inscribed in it, the X its two diagonals, the N two stems plus an inset diagonal. Geometry, not a typeface: nothing to load, identical on every platform, sharp at any size.

Monochrome currentColor throughout, so it tints to any chain accent exactly like the 28 chain marks.

The O overshoots the X and N by 2% — round shapes read smaller than flat ones at equal height. The first draft had them 10.4% too short and the word sagged in the middle.
