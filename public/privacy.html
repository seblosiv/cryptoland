Privacy Policy

CryptoLand · last updated 25 July 2026

What we collect

  • Account data — if you register, your email address and a salted hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Wallet address — if you connect a wallet, its public address. We never receive your private key or seed phrase.
  • Game data — the tiles you claim, their labels and customisation, and in-game activity such as guardians and votes.
  • Usage analytics — pageviews, tile clicks and purchase steps, tied to a random session id, used only in aggregate to understand how the game is used.
  • Payment data — handled by our payment provider. We store the payment identifier and status so we can credit your tile; we never see or store card details.

What we do not collect

No private keys, no seed phrases, no card numbers, and no precise device location. Wallet signatures are used only to prove you control an address.

What is public by design

Tile ownership is public within the game — a tile shows its owner's address or display name, its label, and any image you attach. Do not put anything on a tile that you would not want to be public. Blockchain transactions, where they occur, are public on that chain and cannot be deleted by us.

How we use it

To run the game, process payments, prevent abuse, and improve the product. We do not sell personal data and we do not run third-party advertising trackers.

Sharing

We share only what is necessary with our payment provider and our hosting and infrastructure providers, or where we are legally required to.

Retention and your choices

Account and game data is kept while your account exists. You can request access to or deletion of your account data using the contact address published on the site you are using. Note that data already written to a public blockchain cannot be erased.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage for your session token, your preferences, and a random analytics session id. No advertising cookies are set.
