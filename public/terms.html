1. What CryptoLand is

CryptoLand is a game. The world map is divided into tiles, and players may claim tiles by paying with cryptocurrency. A claimed tile is a record in the game, and — where a supported chain has a deployed contract — may also be represented by a token on that chain.

2. No investment, no guarantee of value

Tiles are game items. They are not securities, shares, or an investment product, and nothing in the game is a promise of profit, resale value, or future utility. Prices shown are what the game charges to claim a tile; they do not represent an appraisal of anything.
